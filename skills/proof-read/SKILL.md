# Proof-read

Determine the intended audience from the request and context. If it is
ambiguous, ask the user and wait for the answer.

Read [Blotch](../blotch/SKILL.md) for human-facing writing or
[Etch](../etch/SKILL.md) for agent-facing writing. Review the requested text
against that skill, including clear spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors.

Report concrete issues with a location or short excerpt. Suggest a correction
when the intended meaning is clear; flag uncertainty rather than guessing.
Say when you find no issues. Do not edit the writing unless asked.
