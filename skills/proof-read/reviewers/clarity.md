# Clarity review

Review the supplied writing against the selected skill. Flag missing or unclear
information only when a competent reader could reasonably misunderstand or
lack what they need to act. Check for clear spelling, grammar, and punctuation
errors. Do not invent requirements.

For each finding, give a location or short excerpt and explain the concern.
Suggest a correction when intent is clear; otherwise, flag uncertainty. Leave
ordinary redundancy to the brevity reviewer. If there are no findings, say so.
Do not edit or delegate.
