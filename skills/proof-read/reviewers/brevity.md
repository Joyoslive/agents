# Brevity review

Review the supplied writing against the selected skill. Identify passages that
can be removed or consolidated while preserving relevant information and
meaning for a competent reader. Consider the context that reader will actually
have, including required references.

For each cut, provide a location or short excerpt and explain how the relevant
information remains available or inferable. Suggest a shorter version when
useful. Report only cuts; leave other findings to the clarity reviewer. If
there are no findings, say so. Do not edit or delegate.
